Skybloom Privacy Policy

Test version. Effective date: 2026-10-24 · Last updated: 2026-10-24
Publisher: Dreamhum, an individual developer in the Republic of Korea · Contact: privacy@dreamhum.com

This policy covers the test versions of Skybloom shared through Apple TestFlight and Google Play testing. Test versions show rewarded video ads from Google AdMob (test ads during testing), which you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and an optional account with cloud save (guest, Google sign-in or Sign in with Apple, Section 2.2). They have no in-app purchases and no analytics services of our own. Before we add either, we will publish an updated policy here and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

1. Who the app is for

Skybloom is a merge-puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed to children. When you first open it, it asks you to choose an age group: under 14, 14–17, or 18 or older. Only the group you choose is kept, on your device. We do not ask for your date of birth or exact age. The age group also decides how ads are requested (Section 2.3).

2. Information the app handles

2.1 Stored only on your device

Your progress and settings (scores, gold, items, skins, collection, language, sound, tutorial status), a game in progress that is saved when you leave the app, a history of your last 20 finished games (for each game: its random seed and starting board, your moves, the final score, highest building and gold, its length, how it ended, and the app version and time; no name, email or account identifier), the age group you chose, and a random guest identifier. The game history is kept for every age group; a new game replaces the oldest one after 20. This stays on your device. We do not receive it. If you chose "under 14" (or did not answer), everything stays on your device and Section 2.2 does not apply.

2.2 Account and cloud save (Google Firebase)

To keep your progress safe and let you continue on another device, the app uses Google Firebase, which we use as our server.

  • Guest (14 and older): when the app can reach our server, a guest gets an anonymous account with a random identifier. No name or email is involved.
  • Google sign-in (optional, 14 and older): if you sign in with Google, Google shares with us your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture, and your guest progress moves to that account. We use your email only to show which account you are signed in with and to answer your requests.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional, 14 and older; iPhone, iPad and the web, not the Android app): if you sign in with Apple, Apple shares with us your Apple account identifier and an email address, and your guest progress moves to that account. You can choose to hide your email; we then receive a private relay address from Apple that forwards to you. Apple shares your name only the first time you sign in, and only if you choose to share it. Apple does not share a profile picture. We use the email and name in the same way as for Google.
  • If you chose "under 14" (or did not answer), the app does not connect to our server: no account, no sign-in, no cloud save and no rankings.
  • Saved on our server: your collection (discovered items and treasures), equipped skins, Decorate layout, game settings, and when they were last updated. Gold is not sent; scores are sent only as game records for rankings (below).
  • Rankings: when you finish a game with an account, the app sends the game (its random seed and your moves) to our server, which replays it to check the score. We keep a record of each game: score, highest building, number of moves, whether the score was verified, app version and time. Your best verified score and your collection count appear on public rankings (top 100, this week and all time), which other players can see, under a ranking name. Only accounts signed in with Google or Apple can choose a ranking name (1–16 characters); guests always appear as "Anonymous". Players who chose "under 14" (or did not answer) are not on the rankings. Please do not use your real name. Your age group is not sent to our server for this. Weekly rankings older than the last 4 weeks and game records older than 90 days are deleted automatically (Section 5).
  • Reporting a ranking name: any player with an account can report another player's ranking name, once per player and up to 5 reports a day. Each report is reviewed by us. When 10 different players have reported a name, it is removed and shows as "Anonymous" on every ranking; the player may then choose a new one. If a name breaks the rules, we may stop that player from setting a ranking name; we decide this ourselves, it does not happen automatically. With the reported account we keep the reported name and the time of each report. We do not keep the reporter's account identifier with the report: to count each reporter only once, we keep a code made from the reporter's and the reported player's identifiers with a secret key on our server, which cannot be turned back into the reporter's identifier without that key. This code stays with the reported account even if the reporter deletes their account. To prevent misuse we keep, with the reporting account, a count of the reports it sent that day. Our server log keeps the reported account's identifier, the name and the number of reporters for 30 days so that we can review reports.
  • When you sign in or save, Firebase also receives your IP address and basic device and app information, which Google uses to run the service and keep accounts secure.

Apart from this and ads (Section 2.3), the app does not send your game data. Its fonts and graphics are included in the app.

2.3 Ads (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch an ad, Google AdMob and Google's ad systems receive, through Google's software in the app:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) can be estimated;
  • device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and performance and crash diagnostics;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • a device identifier, as described below.

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud, and improve its services, under Google's own policies. We do not receive your advertising identifier.

2.4 Optional play log (18 and older)

If you are 18 or older, the app may ask whether you want to join a play test. Nothing is recorded for the play log before you say yes. (The game history in Section 2.1 is separate and is kept on your device either way.) If you agree, the app records on your device:

  • your taps and moves in the game, time on each screen, scores, items, and ads shown;
  • your device situation: platform, operating-system type and major version, app type and version, screen size, language, time zone, memory class, number of processor cores, orientation, and whether reduced motion is on;
  • performance summaries (frame times, load time, error counts);
  • your answers to short surveys, including any text you type (up to 500 characters). Please do not write names, contact details or other personal information.

The record uses random identifiers. It does not include your name, email, phone number, phone model, advertising identifier, or location. Nothing is sent automatically. The record leaves your device only if you create a file in Settings → Send Play Log and share it with us yourself.

2.5 Joining the test

To join a test, you give us the email address you use with Apple or Google, and Apple or Google shares it with us as a tester. Through TestFlight, Apple also gives us crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send, together with your device model and operating-system version. Apple and Google handle this under their own privacy policies.

2.6 What we do not collect

Contacts, photos, precise location, payment details, your date of birth and your password (sign-in is handled by Google or Apple).

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: watching an ad is always your choice. On iPhone you can turn tracking off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings (the menu name depends on the device).
  • Stop the play log: Settings → Send Play Log → Delete and withdraw removes the records and your agreement from the device.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete Account & Data deletes your account, your progress on our server and your data on that device. When you delete an account that uses Sign in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to revoke its sign-in for Skybloom; Apple may ask you to confirm with Face ID or your password first. A Google or Apple account can also be deleted on the web at jincoy.github.io/skybloom-prototype/delete-account.html. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes what is stored on your device, but not an account or progress saved on our server; delete those as above. To delete play-log files you sent us, or to be removed from the test, email privacy@dreamhum.com. We act within 30 days.
  • Access and correction: you may ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, as your local law allows. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

If you choose "under 14" (or do not answer), the app does not connect to our server at all: no account (not even an anonymous guest account), no cloud save, no rankings, no Google or Apple sign-in. Everything stays on the device. The app keeps only the on-device game history of Section 2.1 (last 20 games), which is never sent to us for players under 14. It does not record the optional play log, does not show surveys, does not ask for contact details, and requests ads only as described in Section 2.3: marked as directed to children, limited to general-audience content, without Google's consent message or Apple's tracking prompt. If an account was created on the device before this rule, it can still be deleted (Section 6). If you believe a child under 14 has sent us information, email privacy@dreamhum.com and we will delete it.

8. Security and transfers

Play-log files you send us are kept in access-restricted storage and deleted on schedule. Accounts and saved progress are protected by Firebase security rules, so only you can read or change your own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the authorities as the law requires. We are based in the Republic of Korea. Saved progress is stored by Google Firebase in Seoul; Google, Apple and their partners may process information in other countries.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date above. In-app purchases will not be added until an updated policy is published here. Earlier versions: 2026-10-22 (age groups under 14 / 14–17 / 18+, no accounts), 2026-10-21 (age groups under 13 / 13–17 / 18+, play log from 13), 2026-10-20 (no ads).

개인정보처리방침 요약 (한국어)

이 방침은 TestFlight와 Google Play 테스트로 배포하는 Skybloom 테스트 버전에 적용됩니다. 테스트 버전에는 원할 때만 보는 Google AdMob 보상형 광고(테스트 중에는 테스트 광고)와 선택형 계정·클라우드 저장(게스트, Google 로그인 또는 Apple로 로그인)이 있고, 인앱결제·자체 분석 서비스는 없습니다.

  • 게임 진행, 설정, 나가며 저장된 판, 고른 나이대, 최근 20판 기록(판마다 시드·시작 판, 내 수, 최종 점수·최고 건물·골드, 걸린 시간, 끝난 방식, 앱 버전·시각. 이름·이메일·계정 ID 없음)은 기기에 저장됩니다. 판 기록은 모든 연령에서 저장되고, 20판이 넘으면 가장 오래된 판부터 지워집니다. 만 14세 미만은 이 기록도 저희에게 보내지 않습니다.
  • 진행 보관과 다른 기기에서 이어 하기를 위해 Google Firebase(서울 리전)를 서버로 씁니다. 만 14세 이상 게스트는 무작위 ID의 익명 계정이고, Google로 로그인하면 Google 계정 ID·이메일·이름·프로필 사진을 받습니다. Apple로 로그인하면(iPhone·iPad·웹, Android 앱에는 없음) Apple 계정 ID와 이메일을 받고, 이메일 가리기를 고르면 Apple이 전달해 주는 대리 주소를 받습니다. 이름은 처음 로그인할 때 공유를 고른 경우에만 받고, 프로필 사진은 받지 않습니다. 서버에는 도감(발견한 아이템·보물), 장착 스킨, 꾸미기 배치, 설정, 마지막 저장 시각만 저장하며 골드는 보내지 않습니다. 계정을 삭제할 때까지 보관하며, 90일 동안 쓰지 않은 게스트 계정은 저장된 진행과 함께 자동 삭제합니다(Google·Apple 계정은 제외). 만 14세 미만(또는 나이 미응답)은 서버에 전혀 연결하지 않습니다. 계정(익명 포함)·클라우드 저장·랭킹·로그인 없이 모든 정보가 기기에만 남습니다.
  • 광고를 볼 때 Google이 IP 주소(대략적 위치 추정), 기기·앱 정보, 광고 이용 기록, 기기 식별자를 받아 광고 제공·측정·부정 방지에 씁니다. 저희는 광고 식별자를 받지 않습니다. 동의나 추적을 거절해도 본 광고의 보상은 같습니다. 보상은 광고가 끝까지 나왔을 때만 받고, 광고를 불러오지 못하면(오프라인 등) 그때는 보상이 없습니다.
  • 18세 이상만 Google 동의 안내와 Apple 추적 허용 팝업을 봅니다. iPhone의 광고 식별자(IDFA)는 18세 이상이 "허용"을 누른 경우에만 전달됩니다. 18세 미만과 나이 미응답은 광고 요청에 미성년자·어린이 표시가 붙어 맞춤 광고에 쓰이지 않으며, 만 14세 미만·미응답은 전체 이용가 광고만 받습니다.
  • 만 18세 이상이 동의한 경우에만 플레이 기록이 기기에 남고, 직접 파일을 만들어 공유할 때만 저희에게 전달됩니다. 받은 파일은 30일 뒤 삭제합니다.
  • 추적은 iPhone 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적에서 언제든 끌 수 있습니다.
  • 랭킹: 계정으로 판을 끝내면 판의 시드와 수를 서버로 보내 점수를 다시 계산해 확인하고, 판마다 점수·최고 건물·수의 개수·확인 여부·앱 버전·시각을 저장합니다. 확인된 최고 점수와 도감 수는 랭킹 이름과 함께 다른 플레이어에게 공개되는 랭킹(주간·전체 상위 100)에 나옵니다. 랭킹 이름은 Google·Apple로 로그인한 계정만 정할 수 있고(1~16자), 게스트는 항상 "익명"으로 표시되며, 만 14세 미만·나이 미응답은 랭킹에 나오지 않습니다. 실명은 쓰지 마세요. 이를 위해 나이대를 서버로 보내지 않습니다. 게임 기록은 90일, 주간 랭킹은 이번 주와 그 전 4주까지만 보관하고 자동 삭제합니다. 계정을 삭제하면 모든 랭킹에서 지워집니다.
