Skybloom

Sky Garden Merge Puzzle

  • Playable now on Apple TestFlight
  • Official release planned for mid-November 2026

About the game

Skybloom is my first solo mobile casual game: a sky garden merge puzzle with a futuristic Frutiger Aero and dreamcore art direction.

I compose the music and design the sound effects myself, as part of my artistic practice as a fourth-year BFA student in Composition and Experimental Sound Practices at CalArts.

The game is playable on Apple TestFlight, most of the backend is complete, and the Android build is in progress. Skybloom is an independent project, not a class assignment.

How it plays

  1. Nature, objects, and buildings appear on the board.
  2. Merge matching buildings.
  3. Each merge unlocks the next building in the ladder.
