A garden that grows in the sky

Skybloom is a merge puzzle set on a floating island. Its look is futuristic Frutiger Aero and dreamcore: bright sky, glass, water and soft light.

Unlock 17 buildings, find treasures inside floating bubbles, and dress your garden with new boards, skies and building sets.

The music and sound are original, made for the game.

How to play

  1. 1Place

    Place

    Tap Place to drop the next building on an empty tile. You always see what comes next.

  2. 2 + 2 = 3

    Merge

    Two matching buildings that touch merge into the next level. Up, down, left or right; diagonals don't count.

    • Items
    • Treasures
    • Gold

    Pop bubbles

    Bubbles float up while you play. Pop them for items, treasures and gold.

Rewind

Undo your last move.

Bubble Pop

Clear one building from the board.

Cloud Drop

Choose the tile for the next building.

A 4×4 board and 17 building levels. The game ends when there is no empty tile and nothing can merge.
