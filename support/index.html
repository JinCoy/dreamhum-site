Skybloom

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, also deletes your progress. We cannot restore it for you.

How do I stop or delete the play log?

Open Settings → Send Play Log → Delete and withdraw. This removes the records and your agreement from the device. If you already sent us a play-log file, email privacy@dreamhum.com and we will delete it.

How do I leave the test?

iPhone: open TestFlight, choose Skybloom, then Stop Testing. Android: open the testing link you joined from and choose Leave the program. You can also email us and we will remove you.

Google and Apple sign-in buttons do not work

They are not connected yet in the test version. Please use Continue as Guest.
