Skybloom questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and, if you are 14 or older, also on our server: your collection, equipped skins, Decorate layout and settings are saved to your account, so you can keep playing on another device by signing in with Google or Apple. Gold is not saved on the server. A guest account that is not used for 90 days is deleted automatically, with its saved progress. If you chose “under 14” (or did not answer), everything stays on your device only, and deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I stop or delete the play log?

Open Settings → Send Play Log → Delete and withdraw. This removes the records and your agreement from the device. If you already sent us a play-log file, email privacy@dreamhum.com and we will delete it.

How do I leave the test?

iPhone: open TestFlight, choose Skybloom, then Stop Testing. Android: open the testing link you joined from and choose Leave the program. You can also email us and we will remove you.

Which sign-in can I use?

Continue as Guest, Continue with Google, or Continue with Apple (iPhone and iPad; not in the Android app). Accounts are for players 14 and older. When you sign in with Google or Apple, your guest progress moves to that account, and you can choose a ranking name; guests appear as “Anonymous” on the rankings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Delete Account & Data. This deletes your account, your progress on our server and your data on that device. A Google or Apple account can also be deleted on the web at our account-deletion page. This cannot be undone. Deleting only the app does not delete your account.
